# Product scope

Kaika helps leaders see whether commitments are progressing, understand the work supporting them and act on exceptions. It stops at major deliverables.

Three primary screens: Summary (compact objective portfolio and saved trends), Goal Hierarchy (personal expandable accountability map), and Hygiene (personal actions, incomplete plans, overdue updates and decisions). Goal Charter and Goal Registration are contextual pages. Team allocation belongs to the wider leadership workspace and the relevant goal context.

One canonical goal can be a workstream to its parent owner and an objective to its owner. Pillars group objectives; they carry no completion score. Deliverables are terminal children of workstreams. Support requests share limited context without a second parent or progress credit. Delivery completion and measured outcome attainment remain distinct.

Implemented: approved Goal Charter, five-step registration, delivery/metric/combined success contracts, inheritance acceptance and reasoned pushback, deliverable-linked monthly resources, customizable private avatars and quarterly saved trends. Hierarchy now has shared map/list filters, bounded branches, explicit ancestor context and opt-in reduced-motion-aware glide.

Next acceptance gates: complete save/recovery and accessibility journeys, clearer risk provenance, verified capacity, production identity, scoped SQL and concurrent performance testing. See [the ordered build plan](HIERARCHY-AND-NEXT-BUILD.md). WCAG 2.2 AA remains the documented target; no complete conformance audit has been claimed.
