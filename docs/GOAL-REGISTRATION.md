# Goal registration and setup

Implemented 16 October 2026. This replaces the draft setup presentation with five focused steps while preserving the connected repository, actor scope, private evidence and approved Charter.

## Screen and lifecycle

1. **Register:** create your own commitment or assign a workstream beneath a goal you own. Corporate roots cannot have a parent; department and team relationships use compatible levels. Delegation cannot target someone outside your reporting scope. Direct-link registration initializes the parent choices correctly.
2. **Receive:** the recipient sees the remit before editing. Accept unlocks setup. Pushback requires a reason and creates an action in the assigning leader's Hygiene queue. That leader records clarification and resends; the recipient accepts the same goal identity.
3. **Set up:** Intent & alignment → Success → Breakdown → Resources → Review & submit. Labels, required/optional markers, examples and partial draft saves are available. Cadence is collected with intent. Errors retain inputs and link to the relevant step/field. Save disables editing while the request is in flight.
4. **Submit:** the exact version is locked. The approver can inspect purpose, success rules, metrics, optional operational KPIs, deliverables, checkpoint criteria, people, resource links and dates. Return requires a reason; revision makes a new version and preserves resource-to-deliverable relationships.
5. **Approve:** approved goal links open the Charter. The submitted contract controls progress and evidence acceptance. A proposed revision does not replace an approved version until it is approved.

History stays in the Charter/Hygiene rather than becoming a registration step. Assigned owners complete their own breakdowns; a parent owner can submit a pass-through workstream with dates still unset. Whenever dates are provided, child periods must fit the parent. Deliverables remain final leaves beneath a workstream.

## Success contracts

| Choice | Progress | Completion |
|---|---|---|
| Metric-only | Approved weighted attainment from actual readings, with missing/stale readings identified | Every required target reached with a current observation. No invented deliverable required. |
| Delivery-based | Unique approved leaf effort weighted by accepted checkpoint share | All agreed delivery accepted. No metric required. |
| Combined | Outcome attainment and accepted delivery shown separately | Both gates met. Reaching a metric alone produces “Target met, delivery open.” |

Existing `outcome` contracts retain their combined behavior. Their stored approved versions are not rewritten. New registrations use `metric`, `delivery` or `combined`; editing an old outcome draft selects the equivalent combined contract. Summary filters, metric detail rows and hierarchy labels recognize all three choices. Operational KPI thresholds remain monitoring signals, not completion credit.

## Resource contract and persistence

New setup uses `registration_contract: 2`. Each allocation carries a version ID, deliverable ID, worker ID, work start/end, allocation month and planned percentage. A row represents one named person's commitment to one delivery leaf in one month. Add rows for additional months. The percentage is a monthly commitment and is not prorated for a partial-month date range.

On draft save, deliverable IDs are regenerated by the existing draft writer; resource links are remapped in the same transaction. Revision clones receive new IDs and remapped allocations. Submission and approval validate the links, owner scope, date ranges, finite values and total monthly allocation against existing approved commitments. Draft/proposed copies do not become additional approved workload. Workday capacity is still explicitly unverified.

Older approved goal-level allocations remain labeled and are not retroactively invalidated. When revising them through new setup, the owner must link them to delivery leaves or remove them before submission. Metric-only goals with no delivery have no fabricated resource assignment.

The existing Supabase workshop record payloads store these fields. There is no new public table, permission grant, storage-policy change or browser credential. Commands retain the existing mutation IDs and revision checks. This is still a trusted local persona-switching preview, not authenticated production access or the future fully normalized multi-tenant schema.

## Verification evidence

Two clearly labeled synthetic goals were created in the connected workshop. Imported workforce records were not changed.

- A metric-only goal was created, partially saved, reloaded, submitted without delivery, approved by its designated approver and achieved by a target-reaching observation. Reload retained the result.
- An inherited workstream was pushed back with a reason. The assigning leader saw the Hygiene action, recorded clarification and resent it. The recipient accepted and completed all five steps.
- The combined plan included a 3-person-day deliverable, a 100% checkpoint and a named 10% November resource commitment with dates. The resource-to-leaf link survived save/reload.
- The approver returned v1 for scope clarification. The owner revised to v2, retained the resource link and resubmitted. Approval opened the v2 Charter with the correct resources and dates.
- Reaching the combined metric target kept delivery open. Written synthetic evidence saved as a file in the private bucket, downloaded and was inspected. Independent acceptance then made both requirements complete. Reload and a separate API read verified the result.
- An unrelated leader saw “Goal not available.” The server projection excluded both goals; direct revision and evidence-download requests returned 403.
- An isolated fixture was used for additional delivery-based guidance, keyboard and responsive checks. At 320 CSS pixels, the page had no horizontal overflow and keyboard step navigation focused the step heading. Desktop layout was inspected at 1440px. Browser consoles had no errors.
- Build, typecheck, all UI suites and 56 Python tests passed. Tests cover metric-only completion, combined gates, pass-through assignment, edit restrictions, resource remapping, partial drafts and invalid relationships.

These are scoped functional and accessibility checks, not a full WCAG conformance assessment or production performance certification. The prototype's simulated business date and no-login persona selector remain unchanged.
